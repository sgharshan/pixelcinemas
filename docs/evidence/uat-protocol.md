# UAT protocol

Recruit at least three participants who have not implemented the system. Give each the same task: find *Signal at Midnight*, book any available seat, inspect its approximate view, find the ticket in history, then explain how they would cancel. Observe without coaching. Record completion, time, errors, comments and a 1–5 confidence score. Obtain consent, avoid personal data, anonymise participants (P1, P2, P3), and describe any design change made from the evidence.
